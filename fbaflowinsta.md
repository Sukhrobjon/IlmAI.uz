## Color Palette Recommendation

Based on e-commerce/AI/trust positioning:

| Color | Hex | Usage | Rationale |
|-------|-----|-------|-----------|
| **Primary: Deep Navy** | #1A2B4C | Backgrounds, headers | Trust, professionalism, stands out from orange competitors |
| **Accent: Electric Teal** | #00D4AA | CTAs, highlights, icons | Growth, AI/tech feel, energetic |
| **Secondary: Soft White** | #F8FAFC | Text, cards | Clean, modern |
| **Warm Accent: Coral** | #FF6B6B | Alerts, emphasis | Warmth, approachability |

**Why this palette:**
- Navy = Trust (important for seller tools)
- Teal = Growth + AI/Tech (differentiates from Jungle Scout's orange, Helium 10's blue)
- Avoids: Generic SaaS blue, overused gradients

# 3. VIDEO GENERATION PROMPT

**Purpose:** Hero video showing user journey from frustration → discovery → success  
**Length:** 45-60 seconds  
**Style:** Conceptual/animated, modern motion graphics

## Video Generation Prompt

```
Create a 60-second animated explainer video for FBAFlow, an AI-powered 
Amazon product research platform.

STYLE:
- Modern 2D motion graphics
- Clean, minimal aesthetic
- Color palette: Deep navy (#1A2B4C) backgrounds, electric teal (#00D4AA) 
  accents, white text
- Smooth transitions, subtle animations
- No voiceover (text-on-screen + music)
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (Instagram Reels) + 1:1 (Feed) versions

SCENE BREAKDOWN:

SCENE 1 (0:00-0:08) - THE STRUGGLE
- Character: Simplified avatar of Amazon seller at desk
- Visual: Multiple browser tabs popping up (Jungle Scout, Helium 10, 
  spreadsheets, Amazon)
- Text overlay: "Researching products for Amazon?"
- Emotion: Overwhelmed, stressed
- Animation: Tabs multiply, character gets smaller

SCENE 2 (0:08-0:15) - THE SEARCH
- Character types "mechanical keyboard" into search
- Visual: Flood of 10,000+ results pouring onto screen
- Text overlay: "10,000 results. Zero clarity."
- Animation: Results cascade, character drowns in data

SCENE 3 (0:15-0:20) - THE DISCOVERY
- Visual: FBAFlow logo appears, clears the chaos
- Text overlay: "There's a better way."
- Animation: Logo wipes screen clean, calming effect

SCENE 4 (0:20-0:32) - RESEARCH AGENTS
- Visual: AI agent icon (friendly robot) analyzing data streams
- Show: Products flowing through filter, most rejected, few selected
- Text overlay: "AI Research Agents analyze thousands of products"
- Animation: Data streams into agent, filtered results emerge
- Text: "Find hidden opportunities in seconds"

SCENE 5 (0:32-0:42) - CONTENT STUDIO
- Visual: One winning product selected (keyboard accessory)
- Show: AI generating listing title, bullets, description
- Text overlay: "Content Studio creates launch-ready listings"
- Animation: Text writes itself, optimization checkmarks appear

SCENE 6 (0:42-0:50) - THE DECISION
- Visual: Dashboard with GO indicator lighting up green
- Show: Profit projection, competition score, demand meter
- Text overlay: "Get a clear GO or NO-GO"
- Animation: Confidence meter fills, checkmark stamps

SCENE 7 (0:50-0:60) - CTA
- Visual: Seller avatar now confident, launching product
- Show: Sales graph going up
- Text overlay: "Stop guessing. Start selling."
- Logo: FBAFlow with tagline
- CTA: "Join the waitlist → Link in bio"
- Animation: Smooth fade to logo, subtle pulse on CTA

MUSIC:
- Upbeat, modern electronic
- Builds tension in scenes 1-2
- Resolves to confident, inspiring tone in scenes 4-7
- Reference: Similar to Notion, Linear, or Figma product videos

TRANSITIONS:
- Smooth morphs between scenes
- No harsh cuts
- Consistent easing (ease-in-out)
```
